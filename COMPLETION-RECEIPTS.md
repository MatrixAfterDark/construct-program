# Button-only notifications (current v14)

Final-page arrival no longer sends a viewed notification. The finale cancellation interface is retained as a no-op. Solo Operative sends ATTENDING and plus_one NO; Bringing Backup · +1 sends ATTENDING and plus_one YES to steven.cuzzillo@gmail.com through the same FormSubmit AJAX endpoint as the original Invite. Name comes from the personalised link, otherwise OPERATIVE. No inputs are required.

A confirmed response is saved only after the service acknowledges success. Failed answers stay queued on the same browser for retry; they cannot be transmitted while the site is fully closed. Email delivery is unverified until an actual message reaches the inbox. The guest sees only SELECTION LOCKED after choosing; this acknowledges their local selection and does not claim email delivery.
